The Nonprofits

Our summary in our own words, followed by each organization's official mission statement.

Hunger relief

Atlanta Community Food Bank

The Food Bank collects donated and purchased food and moves it out to hundreds of partner pantries, shelters and community sites across metro Atlanta and north Georgia. Beyond handing out groceries, it works on the root causes of hunger through advocacy and nutrition education, and it depends on thousands of volunteers every year to sort and pack food.

Official mission“Our mission is to fight hunger by engaging, educating and empowering our community.”
Visit acfb.org →
Affordable housing

Atlanta Habitat for Humanity

Atlanta Habitat helps working families become homeowners by building and repairing affordable houses alongside them. Future homeowners put in "sweat equity" hours and take financial classes, and volunteers do much of the building, painting and repair work. Its ReStore thrift shops sell donated home goods to help fund the mission.

Official mission“Community-built, sustainable homeownership.”
Visit atlantahabitat.org →
Food as medicine

Open Hand Atlanta

Open Hand prepares medically tailored meals for Georgians living with chronic illnesses such as diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease and HIV, and delivers them to people's homes. It pairs those meals with nutrition education so clients can manage their health. Volunteers help cook, pack and deliver.

Official mission“We cook. We deliver. We teach. We care.”
Visit openhandatlanta.org →

Volunteer Opportunities

Five ways to serve, with at least one at every organization.

Hunger Action Center Sort & Pack

Atlanta Community Food Bank · 3400 N. Desert Dr., East Point
What you'll do

Work a shift in the Food Bank's main warehouse sorting and packing donated food, repackaging bulk produce and grains into family-size portions, and boxing non-perishable items that go out to partner agencies.

Skills & requirements
  • Ages 8 and up; no experience needed
  • Closed-toe, closed-heel shoes required
  • Arrive 30 minutes early and stay for the full shift
  • Able to stand and lift light boxes
Official description →

Community Food Center Volunteer

Atlanta Community Food Bank · Atlanta (MLK Jr. Dr. SW), Marietta, Jonesboro, Stone Mountain
What you'll do

Help at a neighborhood food center where families pick up groceries directly: sorting food, packing boxes and restocking shelves. The Atlanta center on MLK Jr. Drive is the closest to campus.

Skills & requirements
  • Ages 8 and up; no experience needed
  • Friendly, respectful attitude with clients
  • Closed-toe, closed-heel shoes required
Official description →

Home Build & Paint Crew

Atlanta Habitat for Humanity · Build sites around Atlanta
What you'll do

Join a construction crew working alongside future homeowners to frame, build and paint affordable homes. Site leaders teach the tasks on the day, so you'll pick up real construction skills as you go.

Skills & requirements
  • No construction experience necessary, just a willingness to learn
  • Attend a volunteer orientation before your first shift
  • Comfortable with outdoor, physical work
Official description →

ReStore Volunteer

Atlanta Habitat for Humanity · ReStore locations
What you'll do

Keep Habitat's home-goods thrift stores running: help customers on the sales floor, build merchandise displays, test and clean donated items, and unload donations on the loading dock.

Skills & requirements
  • Customer-service skills for sales-floor roles
  • Creativity and organization for merchandising
  • Able to lift and move items for dock and warehouse roles
  • Watch the orientation video and complete an online questionnaire
Official description →

Meal Packing & Delivery

Open Hand Atlanta · Kitchen and delivery routes
What you'll do

Pack medically tailored meals and market baskets in Open Hand's kitchen, assist the culinary team, help load delivery vehicles, or drive a route bringing meals to clients' homes.

Skills & requirements
  • No experience needed for packing and loading shifts
  • Delivery drivers need their own vehicle and a valid license (confirm on the sign-up page)
  • Individuals and groups welcome; sign up for shifts on the volunteer calendar
Official description →
